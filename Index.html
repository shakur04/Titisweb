Our Story ♡

From one conversation on a friend's phone to becoming something special.

♡

If someone had told me that one ordinary conversation would lead to you becoming such an important part of my life, I probably wouldn't have understood it then. But looking back, I'm glad that conversation happened.

01

APRIL 6

The conversation that started it all

It started when I was talking to Favour, who was my friend first. Then this bright person entered the conversation.

We started talking through Favour's phone, and before long, I asked for your number. The conversation continued, and somehow, one conversation became the beginning of something I wouldn't want to lose.

The beginning ♡
02

A MOMENT I REMEMBER

When you stayed with me

I remember the time I was sick and you stayed with me at the clinic. You could have been somewhere else doing something else, but you were there with me.

It meant something to have you beside me when I wasn't feeling well. It wasn't just about being sick; it was about feeling that I wasn't alone.

You were there 🫂
03

OUR LITTLE SECRET

That first kiss behind the wall

And then there was our first kiss, behind that wall. Just thinking about it makes that moment feel like a little piece of our story that belongs to us.

It wasn't about making a big announcement to the world. It was our moment, and that's what makes it special to me.

Just us ♡
04

AT THE FIELD SIDE

The day I confessed my love

I still remember the day I confessed my love for you at the field side in school. I had feelings I wanted you to know about, and I finally told you.

Some moments become important because of what we say. Others become important because of what we finally find the courage to say. That was one of those moments for me.

I told you how I felt ❤️
05

JULY 11

Officially us

We started talking on April 6, but July 11 was the day we officially became a couple. A date that gave a name to something that had been growing between us.

Since then, we've had more opportunities to learn about each other, make memories, laugh, and keep building our relationship.

My girl. My person. ♡

A little message from me to you

And this is only the beginning.

Titi, I can't promise that every day will be perfect. But I want to keep learning you, listening to you, laughing with you, and making memories with you. I want you to feel appreciated, respected, and safe enough to be yourself around me.

We've already got a beginning worth remembering. I hope we get to look back one day and smile at everything we've been through and everything we've built together.

With love, Shakur ♡
